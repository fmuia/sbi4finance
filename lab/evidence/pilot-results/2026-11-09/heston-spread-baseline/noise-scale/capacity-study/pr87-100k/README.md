# Nested 100k Heston FlowMatching comparison

**Completed: substantially better recovery, but posterior calibration is not ready for an official claim.** The 100k fit improves c coverage and physical point recovery. Both the final epoch 200 and the predeclared minimum-validation epoch 187 still overdisperse physical parameters, disagree with conditional MCMC references and generate draws outside the prior. No checkpoint or tutorial asset is promoted.

The [compact offline report](report.html) is 425,417 bytes and includes both endpoints, per-parameter recovery, selected 90% intervals, SBC, conditional MCMC comparisons, raw support and learning curves. [Standalone scientific figures](scientific-figures/manifest.json) provide PNG and SVG versions. All diagnostics below concern the same reused development panels, not a fresh acceptance test.

## Results on the same 512 synthetic quote sets

The five physical parameters' point RMSEs improve by approximately 41–81%. The final c RMSE falls 17.5%; its nominal-90% coverage is now consistent with 90% in its individual 95% Wilson interval (86.06–91.48%). These are meaningful gains. They do not establish six-parameter posterior accuracy: all five physical marginal SBC screens fail at epoch 200, and each physical 90% coverage estimate is above 90% with its individual Wilson interval excluding 90%. The recovery medians can follow the identity line closely while uncertainty widths remain wrong.

Conditional comparisons ask a different question: for one fixed observed quote vector, does the network match its reference posterior? Across the two ready synthetic joint-MCMC cases, physical central-90% width ratios improve from 3.54–27.49 times reference to 1.11–4.84 times reference at epoch 200. Several means and posterior correlations still disagree. The actual-market c reference concentrates against the upper prior boundary near 2, whereas the network mean is about 1.625. Market fit under the assumed Heston/noise law and inference fidelity are separate questions. The two synthetic references already establish conditional approximation errors without relying on the market case.

The 100-case native validation panel passes all six SBC screens for both endpoints, but has physical 90% coverage of 95–98%. It does not overturn the larger panel or the conditional evidence. Epoch 187 is numerically similar to epoch 200; selecting the validation minimum does not repair the observed calibration. A lower stochastic FlowMatching validation loss is not sufficient evidence of a better posterior.

All raw samples remain in every calculation. In the primary 512-case panel, 16,277 of 262,144 sample vectors leave the prior, including 510 physically invalid vectors; none is nonfinite. In the primary market query, 2 of 4,096 vectors leave the prior (the report's one-decimal percentage display rounds this to 0.0%). No clipping or posterior rescaling repairs these results.

Epochs20/50/75/83/100/150/200 are archived; no other posterior query selects an endpoint. Each evaluated endpoint first repeats the native100-case/300-draw recovery, then uses the exact historical512 quote/truth rows with512 draws per observation, followed by the same three joint-reference cases with4096 draws each. Population observations, truths and posterior seeds match the10k report. All six targets receive recovery, coverage and SBC diagnostics. Existing selected-case MCMC intervals retain the unresolved reference at population index487 without replacement.

Every raw draw is retained, including out-of-prior or nonphysical values. No clipping, rejection, resampling or interval multiplier is applied. Nonfinite draws make a panel's calibration undefined. The report separates prior support from physical validity and keeps conditional mean/width/dependence errors visible alongside pooled calibration. FlowMatching's native stochastic objective is not density NLL; a lower validation minimum does not itself establish posterior accuracy.

## What would support an official claim?

Good recovery plots alone are insufficient. The report checks all six marginal coverage/SBC results, raw prior/physical support, and conditional agreement against ready MCMC. The existing descriptive mean/width conventions are .3 reference SD and ratios .75–1.25. Pearson dependence is additional descriptive evidence, not full-joint equality. Individual Wilson coverage intervals and per-panel DKW screens do not create a new simultaneous acceptance family across endpoints, cases or diagnostics.

Even if these development checks look good, official quality requires freezing a recipe/selection rule and fresh confirmation, with appropriate numerical sensitivity checks and native acceptance tooling before asset promotion. The reserved2000-case pool remains unopened during this comparison. Calibration under the simulator does not establish the market's noise law or market-model adequacy. Grid changes and Bergomi production remain gated.
