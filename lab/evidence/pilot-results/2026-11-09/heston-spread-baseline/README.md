## Fixed-c development continuation

The first c=1 network completed 10,000 finite updates in 163.04 seconds, using the declared 20,000 physical-row prefix. Its 2k and 10k checkpoints reload with identical explicitly seeded posterior draws. All 20,000 rows were seen; 1,273,952 presentations include the short final batches of each pass. The reserved test arrays remain unopened. Compact records are under [training-fixed-c1](training-fixed-c1/); full checkpoints remain outside Git under their original payload hashes.

The first completed conditional comparisons **fail**. On the broad synthetic case, the 10k physical-parameter central-90% intervals are about 4.8-10.0 times reference widths; several means remain outside the declared tolerance. Prior-predictive 90% development coverage is approximately 97.1%-99.8%, indicating over-wide intervals. The actual-market query also fails reference agreement; market-model adequacy is a separate issue. The provisional evaluation files retain incomplete references as inconclusive. Lower validation loss does not accept this checkpoint or permit grid/Bergomi production.

The broad, market-like and actual-market references pass parameter/product convergence and sampled pointwise pricing checks. The boundary reference exhausted its declared 2,700-second wall limit during its one escalation. Its first attempt failed convergence, and retained partial chains are not an accepted reference; comparisons remain inconclusive. The [failure record](references/synthetic-boundary/failure.json) preserves the budget and source bindings. The [local likelihood curvature diagnostic](diagnostics/likelihood-curvature.json) agrees with broad-case MCMC SDs to roughly 2%, with stable finite differences, and verifies the normalized Gaussian scale exactly; it is a local sanity check rather than a posterior replacement.

A [same-state dropout diagnostic](diagnostics/dropout-stateless-update-010000.json) measures a 1.935-nat objective penalty from the inherited dropout .05, while the normalization-stage change is only about .00024 nats. Its exact producer is archived alongside it; reproduce against the frozen trainer source identified in that record (Git commit d7af9c1), not a later changed consumer. No model/optimizer/standardizer/RNG state changed during this diagnostic. The result supports, but does not prove the explanation for, a regularization effect.

The [declared control protocol](dropout-control-protocol.json) allowed one matched 2k/10k fit with effective dropout zero. No noise inflation, prior change, inferred-c fit or geometry change was part of that control.

The [predeclared annealing comparison](annealing-protocol.json) is now complete: absolute updates 50,000-54,999 use 5e-6 and 55,000-59,999 use 5e-7, without resetting Adam or changing the task, bank, noise or architecture. ## What changed

The new `HestonSpreadPilot` uses one independent Gaussian price SD `c * half_spread`. The initial target fixes c=1 and infers five physical parameters. A separate target appends c with the declared illustration prior Uniform(.5,2); c is not a conditioning feature for that target. The implementation includes likelihoods, deterministic seeded simulation, immutable task validation, a normalized one-dimensional conditional-c oracle, bounded native PyMC references, a fixed-layout neural trainer, exact first-failure capture and matched development evaluations. The inferred-c likelihood is implemented but its network is not yet trained.

Checked Fourier pricing is reused from the earlier `HestonPilot`, ported unchanged from historical study commit `78cf9de`. The executing financial model remains in the participant runtime. The new spread-only initializer needs no observed IV or vega and accepts arbitrary finite Gaussian observations, including negative values. Numerical precision remains gated against `.5 * half_spread`, independently of inferred c.

## Software validation and next increment

The full lab suite passed 630 tests. Subsequent focused tests cover matched dropout control, precise failure snapshots, exact zero-dropout checkpoint reload, full-state continuation, bank-resolution interpretation and discarded update-scale diagnostics. One earlier environment-induced runtime-guard failure was traced to an overly strong local source-path overlay, corrected without changing product code or weakening the guard. The deliberate wrong-checkout rejection then passed. Manifest, claims and runtime-boundary release checks pass; tutorial assets are unchanged.

This bounded development round is complete and ready for reflection; no fit is running. Removing dropout and lowering the learning rate materially improve the approximation, but the fixed-c baseline is still unaccepted. More distinct clean surfaces from the preserved 100k bank remain a candidate for improving this particular task; the bank audit motivates that comparison without guaranteeing its result. To attribute the change from the original lesson, first hold its four-dimensional task, prior, geometry and training recipe fixed while varying only a synthetic noise amplitude. The capped boundary reference also needs a separately bounded resolution before full conditional acceptance. No further fit or sampler escalation is launched by these records.

A c=1 checkpoint is not presumed to support other scales. Joint-c training follows its fixed-c control; new-grid labels and Bergomi production remain behind their respective gates. Lower loss, nominal population coverage, or a single good conditional result cannot independently accept the task. The reserved test pool remains unopened throughout this development study.
