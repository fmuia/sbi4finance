Versioned numerical representation

The new-grid checked pricer uses an analytic Black control variate with the unchanged Heston characteristic function, financial priors, quadrature ladder, pricing bounds and measurement-scale precision gates. This addresses cancellation for nearly worthless options without clipping prices or inflating measurement noise. Reference precision checks remain explicit sampled checks, not a prior-wide certificate.

Different observation tasks and optimization budgets. The preserved old54/200k300 control and new63/200k300 each use60 million presentations; new63/300k300 uses90 million. New quote geometry, measured half-spreads and the63-feature condition change the likelihood and network input dimension. More quotes are more independent information only under the assumed independent noise model. The200k→300k comparison combines more simulations with more optimization.

One new63 ZCA matrix is fitted only on the first10k new-grid training rows and frozen across both fits; the old54 matrix is preserved for its own task. All these panels are development evidence; the validation split was used during fitting.
