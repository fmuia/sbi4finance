Revised63-quote Heston grid — 200k simulations ×300 epochs

The supported revised grid uses63 actual contracts, the declared final-call +1.5 standardized target in fixed16% volatility coordinates and an approximately10.7-month longest actual tenor. The canonical Heston priors, normalized Gaussian price likelihood, half-spread noise convention, raw six-target adapter,128×128×128 FlowMatching, dropout0.1 and native AdamW remain fixed.

Versioned numerical representation

The new-grid checked pricer uses an analytic Black control variate with the unchanged Heston characteristic function, financial priors, quadrature ladder, pricing bounds and measurement-scale precision gates. This addresses cancellation for nearly worthless options without clipping prices or inflating measurement noise. Reference precision checks remain explicit sampled checks, not a prior-wide certificate.

Different observation tasks and optimization budgets. The preserved old54/200k300 control and new63/200k300 each use60 million presentations; new63/300k300 uses90 million. New quote geometry, measured half-spreads and the63-feature condition change the likelihood and network input dimension. More quotes are more independent information only under the assumed independent noise model. The200k→300k comparison combines more simulations with more optimization.

Both new fits use the exact same first200k rows of one frozen300k bank. One new63 ZCA matrix is fitted only on the first10k new-grid training rows and frozen across both fits; the old54 matrix is preserved for its own task. Native100 and historical512 six-target truths remain paired with the old control, while the quote vectors belong to the revised grid. All these panels are development evidence; the validation split was used during fitting.

Final epoch300 remains primary. A distinct minimum-validation checkpoint is secondary only. Every raw draw is retained, including values outside the prior; no clipping, rejection, filtering or resampling occurs. Nonfinite draws make whole-panel calibration undefined. Old54 MCMC chains cannot validate the new63 posterior; all conditional comparisons below use new target-matched references.
