# Development evidence — not accepted teaching artifacts

Read the [Oscar development report](../../oscar-pilot-2026-10-26.md) first. These files retain successful, failed and inconclusive development outcomes; they are not a new acceptance registry.

- `readiness-*.json`: actual CPU/GPU execution and storage/recovery checks.
- `heston-development/`: fresh-seed configuration, immutable snapshot metadata, training reports and validation-only diagnostics. Bank payloads and provisional model binaries remain on Oscar.
- `rough-grid/`: all eight initial declarations and reports. Keep each declaration with its report to verify identities/seeds.
- `rough-fine/`: refined component declarations and completed reports. Missing low-H 8× price reports correspond to recorded Slurm timeouts; no price values were imputed.
- `rough-fine-reduced/`: checked local reductions of complete Oscar component sets. No re-pricing or replacement seeds.
- `rough-short-block/` and `rough-short-block-reduced/`: 12 subsequent components and checked reductions, refining only the first maturity block at 4×/8×/16×. Indices 0–17 diagnose that grid; the other quotes remain on fixed grids. The reduced `summary.json` is a convenience extraction from the raw reports, not a new acceptance result.
- `curve-screen/` and `curve-followup/`: fixed-observation challenges, fit budgets, independent audits and point-product comparisons. No posterior uncertainty is claimed.
- `heston-reference-parallel/`: completed PyMC chain/draw arrays, metadata and failing convergence diagnostics. `chains.npz` contains post-warmup samples and original sampler statistics; these are **inconclusive reference runs**, not ground truth.
- `heston-reference-precision/`: selected-point Fourier sensitivity, including hashes of the exact reference inputs.
- `heston-reference-comparison/`: guarded inconclusive outcomes; no neural accuracy verdict was emitted against failed references.
- `heston-reference-conditioned/`, `heston-reference-conditioned-precision/`, and `heston-conditioned-comparison/`: data-only MAP/proposal follow-up on the difficult validation:1 observation. Most parameter checks and both analytic variance-product checks pass, but vol-of-vol still fails R-hat/bulk ESS; the neural comparison remains inconclusive. Original failed runs are retained separately.
- `accounting.psv` and `accounting-summary.json`: terminal task states and reported CPU cost, including timeouts.
- `continuation-jobs.json`, `continuation-accounting.psv` and `continuation-accounting-summary.json`: submitted definitions and terminal accounting for the twelve short-block components, conditioned Heston reference and its Fourier check. The earlier accounting remains separate.
- `pricing-optimization/`: matched one-CPU Oscar benchmarks for conditional-only rough outputs and exact repeated-maturity Heston reuse. Includes raw matched arrays, timings, source/runtime/job identities and accounting; these are performance/equivalence checks, not bank-throughput or numerical-accuracy acceptance.

The PNGs are development figures regenerated by `scripts/plot_pilot_development.py`. They show point fits/coverage, not accepted posterior or product evidence. The final Heston test pool was not evaluated or used to choose configurations.
