## What the next results should decide

1. Retain the matched known50k/100k comparison and review the completed pricing diagnostic and its independent rescore. The original 100k pricer stopped after 116 epochs (selected 86), while 50k completed 300 (selected 298). The new diagnostic keeps the same data, widths and seed, changes only patience, and completes/selects epoch300: validation RMSE falls from .09843 to .07801 quote SD, below the 50k .08287. Fixed-point coarse and doubled-grid errors also improve over both earlier fits at all six points. This supports an optimization explanation; the small reused development panel does not establish global or exact-model accuracy. Neither neural model's case2 posterior meets the predeclared fixed-reference H/eta rules; no posterior was retrained and no new PyMC target was run for this pricer-only diagnostic.
2. Use independent prices to distinguish surrogate error, Monte Carlo variability and grid effects. Keep full batch covariance and paired differences. A refined grid is a comparator, not exact truth; low average pricer RMSE does not rule out damaging conditional errors.
3. Use the small architecture pilot to identify worthwhile follow-ups and measured training/sampling costs. Training losses from different flow families are not comparable scores. This is one-factor screening, not a full family-by-summary-by-seed study.
4. Validate the explicitly named spot-notional forward-start call and analytic variance controls before presenting rough product-price distributions. The legacy return-ratio payoff and a flat curve extension beyond the observed horizon are not substitutes. The product-precision recipe is prepared; reconcile its separate scope/allocation before submission.
5. Review the completed joint20k/100k comparison using the retained validation observations. More data also means about five times as many posterior optimizer updates, and this is one seed. Both joint pricers actually completed 300 epochs, despite the declared patience change from 30 to 300. A rescore against the same saved Monte Carlo batches improves all four fixed-point RMS estimates on both grids; it is a small reused development panel with finite Monte Carlo precision. The joint20k cache remains available until the new results are reviewed. [Independent rescore](pilot-results/2026-10-29/joint100k-independent-rescore/README.md).

Reference convergence, neural/reference agreement, generator precision and downstream pricing answer different questions. Passing one does not imply the others. Keep PyMC where natural, preserve prior/transform Jacobians and chain axes, and label approximate-emulator references accurately.
